# Product FAQ

## Voltage and power
All Brightside electric equipment is 120V and made for use in the United States and Canada. We do not sell 220V models.

## Portafilter size
The ES-200 uses a 58 mm portafilter. Our 58 mm tamper (TP-58) fits the ES-200 and most commercial machines of the same size.

## Grinder burrs
The GR-90 uses 64 mm flat steel burrs. Burrs should be replaced after about 800 lb of coffee, which is roughly 12 to 18 months in a busy cafe.

## Coffee beans
House Blend is a medium roast of Brazilian and Colombian beans. The Decaf blend uses a water process with no chemical solvents. Beans are roasted to order and ship within 3 days of roasting. Unopened bags keep for 6 months.

## Syrups
Syrups come in 750 ml bottles. They are gluten free. Our syrups contain no nuts, but are produced in a facility that also handles tree nuts.

## Cups and boxes
The 12 oz paper cups fit standard 12 oz lids sold separately. Pastry boxes come in packs of 100 and are 10 x 10 x 4 inches.

## Compatibility
The DB-12 drip brewer uses standard number 4 cone filters or 13 x 5 inch basket filters. The IM-40 ice maker needs a cold water line with a 3/8 inch connection.

## Not covered here
We cannot give advice on food safety, health claims, electrical installation or building codes. Please contact a licensed professional for these.
