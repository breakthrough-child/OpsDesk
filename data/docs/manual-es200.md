# ES-200 Espresso Machine Manual

## Specifications
The ES-200 is a dual boiler espresso machine with two group heads. Power is 120V, 1800W. Weight is 62 lb. The water tank holds 3.5 litres and the steam boiler holds 1.8 litres. Pump pressure is 9 bar.

## First use
Rinse the tank, fill with fresh water, and prime the pumps by running the hot water wand until water flows steadily. Wait 20 minutes for the machine to reach temperature. Brew temperature is set to 93 degrees Celsius at the factory.

## Water quality
Use water between 50 and 100 ppm hardness. Hard water voids the warranty because of scale build up.

## Descaling
Descale every 3 months. Use Brightside Descaler (1 litre) mixed 1 to 10 with water. Run half of the solution through the group heads and the rest through the steam wand, then flush with 2 full tanks of fresh water.

## Backflushing
Backflush with a blind basket and cleaning powder at the end of each day. Use 5 cycles of 10 seconds on and 10 seconds off.

## Troubleshooting
Error E1 means the water tank is empty or out of position. Error E3 means the steam boiler is overheating: switch the machine off, wait 30 minutes, and turn on again. If E3 returns, stop using the machine and contact support. If the pump is loud, check the water tank is seated correctly and filled above the minimum line.
