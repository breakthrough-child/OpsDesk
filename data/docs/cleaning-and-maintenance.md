# Cleaning and Maintenance Guide

## Descaler
Brightside Descaler (DS-1L) is a 1 litre bottle of citric acid based descaler. It is safe for all Brightside machines. Do not mix with bleach or other cleaners.

## How often
Espresso machines every 3 months. Drip brewers every month. Ice makers are sanitised every 6 months.

## Water filters
Use a filter if your water is above 100 ppm hardness. Replace filters every 3 months in busy cafes.

## Daily routine
Backflush the espresso machine, wipe the steam wand with a damp cloth after each use, empty and rinse the drip tray, and wash the portafilters.

## Safety
Never open the boiler of a machine that is hot. Do not use a damaged power cord. Unplug equipment before cleaning.
