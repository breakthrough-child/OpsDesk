# Refund and Returns Policy

## Return window
You may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals.

## Non-returnable items
Consumables such as coffee beans, syrups, paper cups and pastry boxes cannot be returned once opened, unless they arrived defective or were not what you ordered. Items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance" cannot be returned.

## Restocking fee
Equipment that has been opened or used is subject to a 15% restocking fee. Unopened equipment in sealed packaging has no restocking fee.

## Return shipping
If the item is defective or we sent the wrong item, we pay for return shipping. For all other returns the customer pays a flat $8.95 return label. Freight items (over 50 lb) are returned by arranged freight pickup and the customer pays a flat $65 freight return fee unless the item is defective.

## Damaged on arrival
Report damage within 7 days of delivery and include photos of the item and the packaging. We will send a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after our warehouse receives the returned item. Shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error or a defective item.

## Refund approvals
Every refund is reviewed by a member of staff before it is issued. Refunds over $150 require manager approval and may take one extra business day.

## Late returns
Requests made after the 30 day window are not eligible for a refund. Staff may offer store credit at their discretion for items returned between 31 and 45 days.
