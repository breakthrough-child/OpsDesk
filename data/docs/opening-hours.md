# Opening Hours and Contact

## Customer support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday 8:00 am to 6:00 pm Pacific Time, and Saturday 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. We are closed on Sundays.

## Showroom and pickup
Our showroom and order pickup counter is at 214 Baker Lane, Maple Falls. It is open Monday to Friday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm and Saturday 10:00 am to 1:00 pm. Order pickup is ready within 2 hours of the "ready for pickup" email.

## Holiday closures
We are closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the day after, and December 24 and 25.

## Response times
Emails are answered within 1 business day. Messages about damaged or defective items are prioritised.

## Contact
Email support@brightside.example or use the chat on the website. We do not take orders or payment details by email.
