# Shipping Policy

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2:00 pm Pacific Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 pm, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day. We ship Monday to Friday.

## Standard shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $7.95. Orders of $75 or more ship free with standard shipping.

## Express shipping
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $19.95. Express is not available for freight items.

## Freight items
Items over 50 lb, including the ES-200 espresso machine and the IM-40 ice maker, ship by freight carrier in 7 to 10 business days. The carrier calls to schedule a delivery window and delivers with a liftgate to the curb. Inside delivery is not included. Freight items cannot be shipped to PO boxes.

## International
We ship to Canada only. Canadian shipping costs $24.95 and takes 7 to 14 business days. The customer is responsible for duties and import taxes. We do not ship to other countries.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when your order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show movement.

## Lost packages
If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, contact us and we will open a carrier claim and send a replacement or refund once the claim is opened.

## Address changes
Addresses can be changed only before an order has shipped. After shipment, contact the carrier to redirect the package.
