# Warranty Terms

## Equipment warranty
All Brightside branded equipment carries a 2 year warranty covering parts and labor from the delivery date. The GR-90 grinder carries a 1 year warranty when used in a commercial setting with more than 100 uses a day.

## Dead on arrival
If equipment does not work when first used, report it within 90 days for a free replacement.

## What is not covered
Consumables, accessories such as tampers and pitchers, cosmetic wear, damage from misuse, and failures caused by not descaling or by using water that does not meet the hardness guidance in the manual are not covered.

## Making a claim
A claim needs the order number, the product serial number, and a short description or photo of the fault. We may ask you to run basic troubleshooting steps first.

## Repair or replace
We decide whether to repair or replace. Shipping for approved warranty repairs is paid by us in both directions. Repairs usually take 10 to 15 business days.

## Out of warranty
After the warranty ends, we offer paid repairs. A quote is given before any work begins. Repair labor is $85 per hour plus parts.
