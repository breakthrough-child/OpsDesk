# Payments, Orders and Cancellations

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. We do not accept cheques or cash on delivery.

## Changing an order
You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it. After that, changes are possible only if the order has not been picked for shipping. A staff member confirms every cancellation.

## Cancelling after shipment
An order that has shipped cannot be cancelled. Once it arrives you can start a return under the refund policy.

## Taxes
Sales tax is added at checkout based on the delivery address. Tax exempt customers can send a certificate to support before ordering.

## Order status meanings
Processing means we have received your order. Shipped means it has left the warehouse and has a tracking number. Delivered means the carrier has confirmed delivery. Cancelled means the order was stopped and refunded.
