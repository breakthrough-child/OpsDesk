# IM-40 Ice Maker Manual

## Specifications
The IM-40 is an undercounter ice maker that makes up to 40 lb of cubed ice a day and stores 12 lb. Power is 120V, 400W. Weight is 58 lb. It needs a cold water line with a 3/8 inch connection and a drain.

## Installation
Leave 3 inches of clearance at the back and sides. Level the unit with the adjustable feet. Installation must be done by a licensed plumber and electrician. We do not offer installation.

## Cleaning
Sanitise the bin and water path every 6 months with ice machine cleaner. Replace the water filter every 6 months.

## Troubleshooting
If the ice is cloudy or tastes bad, replace the filter and clean the unit. If no ice is made, check the water line is open and the bin is not full. A flashing red light means the water supply is interrupted.
