# Equipment Consultation Bookings

## What is a consultation
A free 30 minute consultation with one of our equipment specialists to help you choose and set up equipment for your cafe or bakery. You can choose a phone call, a video call or a visit to the showroom.

## When you can book
Consultations are available Monday to Thursday between 10:00 am and 4:00 pm Pacific Time. Slots are 30 minutes long.

## Rescheduling
You can reschedule up to 24 hours before the slot at no cost. Cancellations or changes with less than 24 hours notice may be declined at staff discretion.

## Cancelling
Cancellations are confirmed by a member of staff. There is no fee for cancelling a consultation.

## What to prepare
Tell us the size of your cafe, the daily volume you expect and your budget. Bring photos of the space if possible.

## Cost
Consultations are free. We do not take any deposit.
