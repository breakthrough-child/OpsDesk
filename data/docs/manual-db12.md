# DB-12 Drip Brewer Manual

## Specifications
The DB-12 brews up to 12 cups (60 oz) in about 8 minutes. Power is 120V, 1500W. It has a stainless steel carafe that keeps coffee hot for up to 2 hours. It has a programmable timer.

## Brewing ratio
Use 2 tablespoons of ground coffee per 6 oz of water, or about 8 level tablespoons for a full 12 cup pot. Use a medium grind.

## Programming the timer
Press PROG and set the clock first. Press PROG again to set the brew start time and then press AUTO. The AUTO light shows the timer is on.

## Cleaning
Wash the basket and carafe after each use. Run a descale cycle every month with 1 litre of Brightside Descaler diluted with 1 litre of water, followed by two cycles of plain water.

## Troubleshooting
If the brewer overflows, the filter is folded over or the grind is too fine. If coffee tastes weak, check the ratio and the grind. Error code H1 means the heating element is too hot; unplug the unit for 10 minutes. If H1 returns, contact support.
