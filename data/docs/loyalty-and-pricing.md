# Loyalty, Pricing and Discounts

## Brightside Rewards
Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent on eligible items. 500 points can be redeemed for $5 off a future order. Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Points are not earned on shipping or tax.

## Price match
If you find the same item at a lower price from an authorised retailer within 14 days of purchase, we will refund the difference. Marketplace sellers and clearance prices are excluded.

## Bulk discounts
Orders of 10 or more units of the same consumable item get 10% off. Equipment is not discounted for quantity.

## Wholesale
Cafes and bakeries can apply for a wholesale account. Wholesale pricing is quoted by a member of the sales team and is not available through support chat.

## Gift cards
Gift cards never expire and are not refundable for cash.
