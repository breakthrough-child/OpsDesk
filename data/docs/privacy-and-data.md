# Privacy and Account Data

## Order information
We only discuss an order with the customer who placed it, identified by the email address on the account. We never share order details, addresses, phone numbers or payment information with another person, even if they are a family member or co-worker.

## Business accounts
For business accounts, additional staff can be added by the account owner. A person who is not listed on the account must ask the account owner to contact us.

## Payment data
We never ask for full card numbers by email or chat. Do not send card details in a message.

## Data deletion
You can ask for your data to be deleted by emailing support. A human member of staff handles all deletion requests within 30 days.

## Retention
We keep order records for 7 years for tax purposes. Support messages are kept for 2 years.
