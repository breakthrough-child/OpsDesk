# GR-90 Grinder Manual

## Specifications
The GR-90 is an on demand flat burr grinder with 64 mm steel burrs. Motor is 120V, 350W. The hopper holds 1.2 lb of beans. It has 40 grind steps from fine to coarse.

## Setting the grind
For espresso start at step 8 and adjust one step at a time. For drip coffee start at step 28. Turn the collar clockwise to grind finer.

## Cleaning
Clean the hopper and burr chamber weekly. Never wash the burrs with water. Use a dry brush or grinder cleaning tablets. Unplug the grinder before cleaning.

## Replacing burrs
Burrs are replaced after about 800 lb of coffee. The burr set part number is GR90-BURR. Replacement is a 20 minute job with a Phillips screwdriver and a hex key.

## Troubleshooting
If the grinder runs but no coffee comes out, the hopper may have a blockage of oily beans: empty the hopper and clear the chute. If the motor stops after a few seconds the thermal cutout has tripped; wait 15 minutes. A burning smell means you should unplug the grinder and contact support.
